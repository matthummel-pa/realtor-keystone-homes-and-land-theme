First-time buyer checklist

Keep this list short enough to scan on a phone. Expand details on separate guide pages when shoppers need depth.

Money first

  • Talk to a lender before touring seriously
  • Know your max payment, not just max price
  • Budget for inspection, appraisal and closing costs

Before you book a showing

  • Filter for must-haves: beds, baths, acreage, commute
  • Save 3–5 homes, not 20
  • Write questions about wells, septic, roof and access

At the tour

  • Walk the lot edges, not just the kitchen
  • Check storage, parking and cell signal
  • Ask what stays with the sale

After you fall for a place

  • Schedule inspection contingencies early
  • Compare payment estimates side by side
  • Decide your walk-away number before writing an offer
